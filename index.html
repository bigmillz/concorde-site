Gatekeeper

macOS builds are not notarized. The first launch will be blocked. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll to the message naming the app, and click Open Anyway. Once per version.

ConcordeVPN servers

ConcordeVPN is bring-your-own-server. There is no network to sign up for. You run your own exit node — the one-command installer ships with the download and sets up the server side, including DNS filtering.

What that gets you is a tunnel you operate end to end. It is not an anonymity tool, and we make no claims about what your server, your host or your network records.
