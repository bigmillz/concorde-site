Install

Direct download

Gatekeeper

macOS builds are not notarized. The first launch will be blocked. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll to the message naming the app, and click Open Anyway. Once per version.

ConcordeVPN servers

Bring your own server — there is no network to sign up for. Three ways to get one:

  1. Automated setupPaste a DigitalOcean token, pick a region and size. The app does the rest.
  2. Command line setupOn a fresh Ubuntu 22.04 or 24.04 server, as root: curl -fsSL https://flyconcordefly.com/concordevpn-server.sh -o concordevpn-server.sh && bash concordevpn-server.sh install Paste the block it prints into the app. concordevpn show prints it again.
  3. Manual setupFor a server you already run: address, three passwords, certificate fingerprint. NextDNS profile ID is optional.

The tunnel is yours end to end. It is not an anonymity tool, and we make no claims about what your server, your host or your network records.

FAQ

10 questions

ConcordeAI

Does ConcordeAI run locally?

Yes. Models download to your computer and run there: MLX on Apple silicon, Ollama on Intel Macs and Windows, using CUDA on NVIDIA cards. Cloud models stay off until you add an API key. Without one, two things still go online: web search, for questions about the world, and image requests, unless local image generation (Apple silicon only) is installed.

How much disk space do I need?

The setup wizard shows each model set's download size for your machine before anything downloads. Basic is one 0.8 GB model. On a Mac, Pro is 7 to 11 GB, and Max, the largest set, is 22 to 25 GB with 16 GB of memory or about 50 GB with 32 GB. Local image generation, Apple silicon only, adds 9.6 GB.

How much memory do I need?

ConcordeAI only lists models your machine can hold: by default, those within 75% of your RAM, or on a PC with an NVIDIA card, 1.25 times the card's memory if that is lower. Loading a model needs about one and a half times its size free, so roughly 4 GB for Llama 3.2 3B and 12 GB for Gemma 4 12B. If a model can't load, a smaller installed one answers.

Why use it over an online AI tool?

With no cloud key, text answers come from models on your computer, your chats are saved in a file on it, and local models work offline. Add a Gemini, Groq, Claude or Kimi key and cloud models join in. Thinking and Pro modes blend several models' drafts into one answer, and the same window has a Code lane, with an SSH agent that asks before irreversible commands, and Funnels for step-by-step decisions.

ConcordeVPN

Is a Windows version coming?

Not at the moment. ConcordeVPN is macOS only and no Windows version has been announced: it is built from macOS parts, from the background service that runs the tunnel to the menu-bar app itself.

What does it do that other VPN software doesn't?

It has three ways through, Trojan, VLESS + REALITY and Hysteria 2, and when one stops answering it moves to the next. REALITY answers a probe with the real website whose certificate it borrows. The app names your exit only after checking your public address, has a built-in leak test, and from 1.4 has a kill switch that stops internet traffic leaving outside the tunnel.

Why do I need to run my own server?

ConcordeVPN is only the client: there is no ConcordeVPN network or account to sign up for. The tunnel ends at a server you run, where the installer generates the passwords and keeps the system log in memory only. The app can also create one on DigitalOcean for you. It is not an anonymity tool: the server sits in your own hosting account.

Why use it over other flight search tools?

Every flight site sells the same flights. ConcordeGo ranks them by what the trip costs door to door, in dollars: the ticket, bags the fare leaves out, rides to and from the airports, layovers, your time and comfort. Each flight gets a letter grade on six rubrics, with price judged against a fair fare for that route and date. Same search, same data, same order.

What's the Flight Fixer?

It is for a flight that is delayed or canceled. It searches the day's other flights and prices each one: the ticket, less any refund you may get, less the time it saves at your hourly rate, plus an estimated hotel night and rides if it leaves tomorrow. It says switch, stay or wait, estimates your odds of flying out today, and lists what you may ask the airline for.

How is the all-in cost worked out?

All in is what you pay: the ticket with taxes and fees, checked bags the fare doesn't include, rides to and from the airports, a hotel night when a layover runs overnight, and extras you ask for, such as a wifi pass. Rides are estimated from your address and the time of day, and lean high; an unpublished bag fee is priced high, never free. Your time and comfort change the order, not the bill.

Why they exist

I built these to fill gaps: things the apps I already used couldn’t do, and tools I needed that didn’t exist. Once they worked well enough for me every day, I cleaned them up so other people could use them too.

This is a hobby, not a business, and I’m adding features all the time. If one of these turns out to be useful to you, keep it up to date. ConcordeAI and ConcordeVPN update themselves; ConcordeGo is a website, so it is always current.
